Why we started Matrix Math

We got tired of watching capable students decide they were "just not a math person" over one bad unit. Matrix Math exists to catch that unit before it turns into a story a student tells about themselves for years. Every tutor here has taught long enough to recognize the difference between a student who's confused and one who's given up — and to know that those need very different responses.
